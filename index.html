How it works
  1. Pick a card from your hand, then click a gap in the bottom row — including between two cards already played; the rest shift right.
  2. Link: the suit drawn on your card's edge (top/bottom/left/right) matches the suit of the neighbour on that side → you take that neighbour's current power.
  3. After every card played, the whole row is re-checked — any new neighbour pair that lines up pays out, including cards that got pushed around.
  4. A link pays only once per pair. Power gained is kept — it becomes the card's new base power.
  5. Build row = deal the row's total as damage. The row stays on the carpet and the next one can link up into it.
